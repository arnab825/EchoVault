# Archival Photograph Context: St. Mark's Steps, Venice
# Date: circa June 1961 (Approximate)
# Medium: Silver Gelatin Print, 4x6 inch, Matte

Physical Description:
Black-and-white silver gelatin print preserved in brown buckram album, page 14.
Shows great-aunt Eleanor sitting on the white stone steps of St. Mark's Basilica in Venice.
She wears a pleated cream linen dress and sunglasses with cat-eye frames.
Beside her sits uncle Julian, adjusting the lens of an Eastman Kodak folding camera.

Graphite Inscription on Reverse:
"St. Mark's steps, Venice, early summer 1961. The pigeons were relentless, but Julian insisted on the morning light before the gondolas crowded the canal."
