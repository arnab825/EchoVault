# Letter from Florence to Arthur
# Date: March 14, 1944
# Location: Rosewood Cottage, Sussex

March 14, 1944 — Dearest Arthur,

Spring has arrived timidly in Sussex. The daffodils near the garden stone wall opened their pale yellow petals yesterday morning. 
The postal carrier said trains from Bristol have been delayed by heavy morning fog, but I watch the lane every afternoon for your post. 

Mother repaired your tweed overcoat, replacing the worn bone buttons with dark horn ones she had saved from grandfather's trunk. 
We have tucked away two tins of sweet peaches for your homecoming feast. 
The orchard trees have begun to bud; if the frost stays away, we will have a bountiful crop of damsons and pears this autumn.

Stay safe in the depot, and know that you are in our prayers each evening.

With all my love,
Florence
