# The Autumn Lantern Walk along the River (October 15, 1952)
# Author: Grandfather Julian Linnea
# Location: Oakhaven Riverbanks

In the autumn of 1952, grandfather took all of us down to the river for the annual harvest lantern walk. 
The water was glassy and reflected hundreds of amber paper lanterns drifting toward the bay. 
Aunt Clara brought warm spiced apple buns wrapped in checkered linen. We walked hand-in-hand along the cobblestones 
until the clock struck nine, when the brass band played by the boathouse.
Uncle Thomas brought his accordion, playing sea shanties that made the children laugh.
The air was brisk, scented with woodsmoke and fallen oak leaves.
