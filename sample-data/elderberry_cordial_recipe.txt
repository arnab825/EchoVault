# Handwritten Recipe: Elderberry Cordial for Winter Chills
# Date: circa 1938 or 1939 (Ambiguous / Approximate)
# Document Source: Loose leaf tucked inside Family Bible

Ingredients & Method:
Gather three quarts of ripe elderberries after the first hard frost.
Strip berries cleanly from stems using the tines of a silver fork (never pewter or iron).
Simmer gently over low coals with twelve whole cloves, crushed cinnamon bark, and thin slices of bitter Seville oranges.
Strain through unbleached calico into an earthenware jug.
Stir in two pounds of raw sugar and one pint of fine French brandy while still lukewarm.
Cork tightly and wax the stoppers. Store in a cool cellar until Martinmas.

Marginalia in faded sepia ink:
"Mother prepared two flagons of this cordial before the hard frost of 1938, or perhaps early 1939 when little Clara had the winter pleurisy."
