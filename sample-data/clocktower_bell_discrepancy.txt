# Discrepancy Record: Repair of the Village Clocktower Bell
# Sources: St. Jude's Parish Council Minutes vs. Uncle Thomas's Daily Journal
# Subject: Disputed installation date of the replacement church bell

RECORD 1 — St. Jude's Parish Council Minute Ledger (Official Record):
"Meeting of the Vestry, September 1, 1923:
The grand tenor bell, having been fractured during the severe westerly gale of November 1922, was received back from the Whitechapel Bell Foundry in good order. 
Following a service of solemn benediction presided over by Father Martin, the bell was hoisted into the belfry and rung for evening vespers on the first of September, 1923."

RECORD 2 — Personal Journal of Thomas Linnea (Eyewitness / Contrarian Account):
"Entry dated October 18, 1925:
They finally hauled the replacement clocktower bell up the ropes this morning after two years of unseemly bickering over the blacksmith's bill. 
The council claimed it was done in '23, but the bell sat in the timber yard under canvas until the parish debt was settled last Michaelmas. 
Poor Father Martin was already confined to his sickbed, so young curate Davis offered the blessing in the rain."
