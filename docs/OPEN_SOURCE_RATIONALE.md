# Open-Source AI Rationale: EchoVault

## Why Open-Source AI for Memory Preservation?

Digital preservation is fundamentally a multi-generational commitment. Proprietary AI services run counter to long-term digital preservation for three structural reasons:

### 1. The Perils of Model & API Sunset
Commercial cloud AI services deprecate endpoints, change models, and alter safety filters frequently. If your personal archive depends on a commercial API, your archive can break when that service raises prices, alters terms of service, or shuts down. Open-weight models (like Llama 3.2 and Qwen 2.5) can be archived, stored on offline hard drives, and run decades from now on future hardware.

### 2. Intimate Family Sovereignty
Family memories include hospital records, wartime letters, love notes, financial disputes, and confidential oral accounts. Forcing families to upload these treasures to proprietary third-party corporations creates unacceptable surveillance risks. Open-source local inference keeps private memories inside the home.

### 3. Transparent Evidence over Black-Box Hallucination
In commercial chatbots, system prompts and temperature settings are opaque. In EchoVault, the retrieval pipeline, prompt boundaries, and citation resolution algorithms are completely open-source. Anyone can audit how answers are synthesized and verify that citations map to real historical sources.
